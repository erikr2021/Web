A local team built on speed, honesty and results — protecting the homes and businesses of Delaware and Pennsylvania.

Our Mission

Fast Relief, Lasting Protection

Rapid Pest & Wildlife Solutions was founded on a simple idea: when pests or wildlife invade your space, you shouldn't have to wait days for help or settle for a temporary fix. Our mission is to deliver fast, effective, and humane solutions that solve the problem at the source — and keep it solved.

We treat every home and business like it's our own. That means showing up on time, explaining exactly what we're doing, using the safest effective methods available, and standing behind our work with a satisfaction guarantee.
