What We Do

Four steps to a pest-free home.

From the first trap to the final seal, we handle the whole job — so the problem is solved once, not patched over.

Trapping

Targeted, humane trapping and removal of raccoons, squirrels, bats, opossums and rodents — sized to the animal and set where they're actually moving.

  • Live & humane
  • Attic & crawlspace
  • Rodent stations

Exclusion

We find every gap, vent and roofline weak point they used to get in, then reinforce it with steel and pro-grade materials that keep wildlife out for good.

  • Entry-point audit
  • Steel mesh & flashing
  • Vent & chimney caps

Seal-Up

The finishing work that makes it last: gaps caulked, holes patched, damaged soffit and fascia rebuilt so your home is buttoned up tight and weatherproof.

  • Gap & crack sealing
  • Soffit & fascia repair
  • Weatherproofed

Maintenance

Ongoing inspections and monitoring on a schedule that fits your property, catching new activity early — before it turns into another emergency.

  • Scheduled visits
  • Bait station checks
  • Priority booking
